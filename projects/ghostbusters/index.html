Strap on a proton pack and recover cursed artefacts across a haunted New York.

The game

In Ghostbusters: The Cursed Collection, players join the team in a New York overrun by spirits. They recover cursed artefacts, catch mischievous ghosts and take on Stay Puft, with proton streams aimed by body tracking.

The HUD frames the game as a live broadcast, with a score, a viewer count and a marker for each player's stream. Boss health bars keep the whole group aiming at a shared target.

What I did

  • Designed the game, its levels and the boss encounters
  • Designed the HUD and interface for six players aiming at the same wall
  • Prototyped and playtested the aiming and capture mechanics
