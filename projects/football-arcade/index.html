LV 06

Football Arcade

A limited-time football festival: keepy-uppies, team battles and streaks, played with your whole body.

The game

Football Arcade was a limited-time run of football skill games for the world's biggest football tournament. Teams go head to head in games like Keepy Uppy, with scoreboards that are big enough to read mid-game: team scores, a timer, and current and best streaks.

What I did

  • Designed the collection of football skill games
  • Designed the room-scale scoreboard and HUD
  • Prototyped and playtested the ball physics and team play
