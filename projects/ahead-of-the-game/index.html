The Challenge: a mental-health resilience learning tool for young athletes, built around interactive video.

The game

Ahead of the Game is Movember's mental-health program for sport. It teaches athletes, parents and coaches how to talk about mental health, recognise when someone is struggling, and know when to get help.

The Challenge is a mental-health resilience learning tool built around interactive video. Young athletes watch players face high-pressure moments, work out what they might be thinking, sort helpful thoughts from unhelpful ones, and practise strategies like a breathing exercise to keep their cool, on the field and off it.

It's the program's companion app for athletes, carrying on from the facilitated workshops.

What I did

  • Designed the learning experience for The Challenge, carrying on from the program's group workshops
  • Worked with men's psychologists to get the content right
  • Worked with Movember's development and creative teams
