A mental-health resilience program for young men, combining in-person and digital experiences.

The game

Ahead of the Game teaches young men strategies for resilience. It pairs an augmented group activity run in person with a self-directed mobile app.

What I did

  • Designed the learning experience across the group activity and the app
  • Worked with Movember's development and creative teams
  • Worked with men's psychologists to get the content right
