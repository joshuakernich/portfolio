LV 07

Box Party

Client
Immersive Gamebox
Role
Lead designer · Director of Experience Design
Players
Up to 6
  • Motion tracking
  • LiDAR touch walls
  • Projection

Battle, race and puzzle through wild, physical party games to top the leaderboard.

The game

Box Party is a collection of fast, highly physical party games, with different games each visit. Groups battle, race and puzzle their way up the Gamebox leaderboard.

What I did

  • Designed the party games and how the session flows between them
  • Designed the leaderboard and competitive structure
  • Prototyped and playtested each game with real groups
