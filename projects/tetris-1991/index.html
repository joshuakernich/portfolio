The game

A tech genius hacks a handheld Tetris game, and suddenly everything and everyone is turning into Tetriminos. Groups of up to six play five human-sized Tetris mini-games solo, in pairs and together to put the world back to non-cubular shapes.

The look is authentic early-90s handheld. The challenge was keeping the retro feel while staying readable on a wall-sized screen in the middle of a physical game.

What I did

  • Designed the end-to-end storyboard, games, narrative and player experience across five mini-games
  • Created and tested a range of prototypes for the key mechanics of human-sized Tetris
  • Set the 1991 art direction, and guided art, animation and technical implementation throughout
  • Managed the development team from design through to delivery
