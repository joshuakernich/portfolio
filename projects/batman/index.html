Batman

Client
Immersive Gamebox
Role
Lead designer · Director of Experience Design
Players
Up to 6
  • Motion tracking
  • LiDAR touch walls
  • Projection

A one-hour co-op adventure in the Batcave: solve puzzles, battle villains and drive the Batmobile to save Gotham.

The game

Batman is a full-hour cooperative adventure for a Gamebox room. Players team up in the Batcave to solve puzzles, fight Gotham's villains and take the wheel of the Batmobile, all through 3D graphics and animated storytelling that respond to their movement around the room.

What I did

  • Designed the game from start to finish and wrote the full design documents
  • Built high-fidelity prototypes to test puzzles, fights and the Batmobile section
  • Directed the art and supported the development team through production
  • Worked directly with Warner Bros and the Batman design team through approvals
