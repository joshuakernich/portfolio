The Thought that Counts

Client
Own project, with Kitney Hartstone Productions
Role
Designer & developer
Year
2021
  • Arcade installation
  • Group play

A mental-health arcade game where your group decides what a pixel bunny thinks.

🏆 Weekly prize for Best Interactive Art, Adelaide Fringe 2021 · $10k SA Arts grant

The game

In The Thought that Counts, a group plays together to choose the thoughts of a pixel bunny going through a mental-health crisis. Players see how their choices shape its view of the world, how it falls into despair, and how friendship and love can pull it back.

It ran as a pop-up installation at Adelaide Fringe and Adelaide City Library.

What I did

  • Designed and programmed the whole game
  • Won a $10,000 SA Arts grant to fund it
  • Worked with a production team in Adelaide on the physical installation
